Is this the real engine?

It is a faithful mirror of the documented behaviour, and it is held to the project's own decisions. The site's test suite feeds this engine the policies from safeguard-policy and asserts that the (decision, reason code, rule id) triples it produces are exactly the ones recorded in the upstream golden fixture — crates/safeguard-sdk/tests/fixtures/decisions.json.

The authoritative implementations remain the Rust engine in safeguard-policy/crates/safeguard-core (off-chain) and the Soroban contract in safeguard-policy/crates/safeguard-contract (on-chain). This page exists so you can see the semantics without cloning, building or funding a wallet.

This demo does not touch the deployed Testnet contracts. The authoritative state lives on-chain at the addresses listed on the contracts page; evaluation against the live contract additionally requires the subject's registry state, which the contract resolves itself.
